What Quarterback does

A checklist before the work, evidence after it.

AI coding assistants are fast, but their changes can look finished while missing part of what was asked. Today someone has to read every line to be sure. Quarterback adds structure around the assistant so you can see what was checked and how it turned out.

  1. 01

    Agree on the requirements

    A short list: what must be true, which files may change, and checks that can be run automatically. You approve it. The coding assistant doesn’t start before you do.

  2. 02

    The AI works in a separate workspace

    Claude Code makes the change on a copy of your project, inside an isolated container with limited network access. Your own files stay as they are.

  3. 03

    Quarterback checks the result

    It runs your project’s tests and the agreed checks on exactly what changed. A pass means those checks passed. It doesn’t rule out every possible bug.

  4. 04

    You review and decide

    You get the outcome, the evidence behind it and the change itself. Nothing is applied to your project unless you choose to apply it.
